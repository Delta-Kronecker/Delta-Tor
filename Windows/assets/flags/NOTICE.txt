Country flag images in this folder (flags/*.png)

Source: https://flagcdn.com (flag artwork derived from Wikipedia /
Wikimedia Commons, which are free to use).
Terms: flagcdn.com offers these flag images free for personal and
commercial use.
Codes follow ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, matching countries.tsv in assets/geoip/.
They are rendered by the app instead of flag emoji, which show as plain
letters on Windows machines without the emoji font.
